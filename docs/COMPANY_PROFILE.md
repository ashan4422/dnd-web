# Company Profile — Dee & Dee Lanka (Pvt) Ltd

_Researched 2026-09-29 from public sources. Items marked ⚠️ conflict between sources and need confirmation._

> Note: the brief said "Dee & Dee Lank"; every public source uses **Dee & Dee Lanka (Pvt) Ltd** (brand/domain: **D&D Lanka / dndlanka**).
> No saved Claude memory about the company was available in this session — everything below is from the web.

## Summary
- **Business:** Importer, inventory holder and distributor of bearings, oil seals, hydraulic seals and related products, for automotive and industrial use. Industry on LinkedIn: Wholesale.
- **History:** Started in **1990** as a sole trading business; became a limited liability company in **2005**.
  - ⚠️ TradeIndia says established **1982**; LankaYP says registered **27 June 2012**.
- **Stock range:** "more than **18,000** items" (current website). ⚠️ LinkedIn says 17,000+.
- **Size:** 11–50 employees (28 on LinkedIn).
- **Tagline on current site:** "Bearings & Sealing Technologies for Automotive and Industrial Applications".

## Vision & mission (from the current website)
- **Vision:** "To equip our strategic partners with effective and efficient knowledge based solutions, to enable them to have trouble free operation anywhere, at any time."
- **Mission:** "To be the most consistent, preferred and trusted company."
  - Customers: high-quality components and knowledge-based solutions to boost productivity
  - Employees: a supportive workplace where contributions are valued and respect is kept
  - Shareholders: sustainable profit growth while strengthening the company's reputation
